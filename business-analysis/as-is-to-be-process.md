# As-Is vs. To-Be Process Analysis

## 1. Project Information

- **Project:** Nova Bank — Mobile Banking Application Enhancement
- **Role:** Business Analyst
- **Domain:** Digital Banking
- **Document Type:** Process Analysis
- **Status:** Portfolio Case Study

## 2. Purpose

This document compares the assumed current customer journey with the proposed improved mobile banking process.

**Disclaimer:** Nova Bank is a fictional portfolio case study. The current-state challenges are assumptions, not verified findings from a real bank.

## 3. As-Is Process — Current Assumed Journey

1. Customer opens the mobile banking application.
2. Customer attempts to access an account or banking service.
3. Customer encounters a registration, payment, or transaction-status issue.
4. Customer checks transaction details but may remain uncertain about the outcome.
5. Customer may seek additional support.
6. The issue is resolved after the transaction status is confirmed or assistance is provided.

### Key Pain Points

- Registration difficulties.
- Failed or pending transactions.
- Unclear transaction statuses.
- Difficulty checking transaction updates.
- Potentially avoidable support enquiries.

## 4. To-Be Process — Proposed Improved Journey

1. Customer registers and completes required verification.
2. Customer logs in securely.
3. Customer views account balance and transaction history.
4. Customer selects a beneficiary or supported biller.
5. Customer enters transaction details and completes required authorization.
6. The system processes the request and displays Success, Failed, or Pending.
7. Customer receives notifications for supported transaction events.
8. Customer accesses self-service help when needed.

### Proposed Improvements

- Guided registration and validation.
- Clear transaction status messages.
- Easy access to transaction history.
- Notifications for supported transaction events.
- Self-service guidance for common questions.

## 5. Process Comparison

| Area | As-Is: Assumed Experience | To-Be: Proposed Experience |
|---|---|---|
| Registration | Customer may face difficulties | Guided registration and validation |
| Fund transfers | Outcome may be unclear | Clear transaction status |
| Bill payments | Payment issues may need clarification | Payment validation and status visibility |
| Transaction history | Customer may struggle to find updates | Accessible transaction history |
| Notifications | Updates may be unclear or unavailable | Notifications for supported events |
| Support | Customer may need assistance | Self-service guidance is available |

## 6. Expected Business Benefits

- Better visibility into transaction outcomes.
- Easier access to digital banking services.
- Reduced uncertainty about pending transactions.
- Improved access to transaction information.
- Potential reduction in avoidable support enquiries.

These benefits are proposed outcomes and have not been measured.
