# As-Is vs. To-Be Process Analysis

## 1. Project Information

- **Project:** Nova Bank — Mobile Banking Application Enhancement
- **Role:** Business Analyst
- **Domain:** Digital Banking
- **Document Type:** Process Analysis
- **Status:** Portfolio Case Study

## 2. Purpose

This document compares the assumed current mobile banking experience with the proposed improved process.

**Note:** Nova Bank is a fictional case study. The As-Is process represents assumed problems, not a verified process from a real bank.

## 3. As-Is Process — Current Assumed Experience

1. Customer opens the mobile banking application.
2. Customer attempts to log in or access a banking service.
3. Customer encounters a registration, payment, or transaction-status issue.
4. Customer may be unsure whether a transaction succeeded.
5. Customer checks transaction details or seeks support.
6. The issue remains unresolved until the customer receives confirmation or assistance.

### Potential Pain Points

- Registration difficulties.
- Failed or pending transactions.
- Unclear transaction status.
- Limited visibility into transaction updates.
- Potentially avoidable support enquiries.

## 4. To-Be Process — Proposed Improved Experience

1. Customer registers and completes the required verification.
2. Customer logs in securely.
3. Customer views the account balance and transaction history.
4. Customer selects a beneficiary or supported biller.
5. Customer enters the transaction details and completes required authorization.
6. The system processes the request and displays Success, Failed, or Pending.
7. Customer receives a notification for supported transaction events.
8. Customer accesses self-service help if further assistance is required.

### Proposed Improvements

- Clearer registration and login steps.
- Validation before transaction submission.
- Clear transaction status messages.
- Better visibility into transaction history.
- Notifications for supported transaction events.
- Easier access to self-service support.

## 5. As-Is vs. To-Be Comparison

| Area | As-Is: Assumed Experience | To-Be: Proposed Experience |
|---|---|---|
| Registration | Customer may face difficulties | Guided registration and validation |
| Fund transfer | Customer may be uncertain about the outcome | Clear transaction status |
| Bill payment | Payment issues may require clarification | Validation and status visibility |
| Transaction history | Customer may need to search for details | Accessible transaction history |
| Notifications | Transaction updates may be unclear or unavailable | Notifications for supported events |
| Support | Customer may need assistance for common queries | Self-service guidance and support options |

## 6. Expected Business Benefits

- Improved customer visibility into transaction outcomes.
- Easier access to essential banking services.
- Reduced uncertainty around pending transactions.
- Better access to transaction information.
- Potential reduction in avoidable support enquiries.

These are expected benefits, not measured results.
